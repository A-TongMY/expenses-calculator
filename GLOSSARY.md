# Expenses Calculator

A personal tool that shows how much of a monthly income is left after expenses, and whether a prospective new commitment can be absorbed while keeping the current lifestyle.

## Language

**Income**:
The single fixed monthly amount of money coming in.
_Avoid_: Salary, budget, earnings

**Expense**:
Any money going out each month. The umbrella term for every kind of outgoing.
_Avoid_: Cost, spending (as the umbrella)

**Commitment**:
A fixed-amount obligation, either recurring indefinitely or ending on a date.
_Avoid_: Bill, fixed expense, obligation

**Lifestyle spending**:
A discretionary Expense you could choose to cut, such as food, fuel or a salon visit. It may be irregular and is entered as a range.
_Avoid_: Daily spending, variable expense, other

**Monthly cost**:
The amount an Expense contributes to a single month, after spreading it over its cadence (a 150 MYR visit every 3 months costs 50 a month).
_Avoid_: Amortized amount, average

**Check-in**:
An optional month-end entry of what was actually spent per Lifestyle spending item, used to compare against its estimate.
_Avoid_: Log, transaction, reconciliation

**Scenario**:
A Commitment being considered but not yet real. It can be switched on or off to see its effect, and promoted to a real Commitment.
_Avoid_: What-if, draft, simulation, prospective commitment

**Projection**:
The month-by-month Remaining over the coming months, honouring each Expense's start and end dates.
_Avoid_: Forecast, timeline

**Remaining**:
What is left of the Income after all Expenses, shown as a range from best case to worst case.
_Avoid_: Balance, surplus, savings
